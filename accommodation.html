The essentials

Six things to know before you book.

35,000

Partner rooms

Partner hotels have frozen their rates at 2029 prices for the whole of the Games. Look for the KL 2030 label when you book.

Book early

Hotels within walking distance of Bukit Jalil are expected to be full by spring 2030. The city centre has far more choice.

Stay near a station

Pick a place within ten minutes' walk of an LRT, MRT or Monorail station: the roads around the venues are jammed on finals nights.

Your ticket is a travel pass

On the day of your event, your ticket gives free travel on the LRT, MRT, Monorail and Games shuttles.

Registered holiday lets only

Holiday rentals must display a city registration number. No number, no booking: it protects you from scams.

Accessible rooms

Partner hotels list their step-free rooms, and the official booking platform lets you filter for them.

    Types of accommodation

    Indicative prices per night during the Games.

    • Hotels

      From international chains around KLCC to small boutique hotels in Chinatown shophouses. Breakfast is often a buffet of nasi lemak, roti and noodles.

      RM 120–1,500

    • Serviced apartments

      A Kuala Lumpur speciality: flats with a kitchen, a washing machine and often a rooftop pool, rented by the night. Ideal for families and groups.

      RM 200–600

    • Homestays and holiday lets

      Registered rooms and houses, from the wooden houses of Kampung Baru to suburban terraced homes. A good way to meet local families.

      RM 100–400

    • Hostels and capsule hotels

      Dorm beds and sleeping capsules, mostly in Chinatown and Bukit Bintang. Many have rooftop bars and organise group outings to the events.

      RM 40–150

    Arriving in Kuala Lumpur

    By train: the KLIA Ekspres takes you non-stop from both airport terminals to KL Sentral in 28 minutes. The KLIA Transit makes the same trip with stops, including Putrajaya.

    By road: allow about an hour by taxi or ride-hailing app to the city centre, more in the evening rush hour.

    Welcome desks: KL 2030 desks in the arrivals halls hand out transport maps and help you reach your accommodation, from 6 am to midnight throughout the Games.
