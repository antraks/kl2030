Visitor info

Everything you need to know to enjoy the Games: entering Malaysia, coping with the heat, what to bring to the venues and how to get around the city.

Before you travel At the venues FAQ

Before you travel

Entry rules change: check them with official sources for your nationality before you book.

999

Emergency number

Police, ambulance and fire brigade. From a mobile phone, 112 also works.

Passport and visa

Your passport must be valid for at least six months. Citizens of many Commonwealth countries, including the UK, Australia, Canada and New Zealand, need no visa for a short stay.

Digital arrival card

Fill in the Malaysia Digital Arrival Card online in the three days before you arrive. It is free: ignore websites that charge for it.

Currency

The Malaysian ringgit (RM). Cards are widely accepted and cash machines are everywhere, but small street stalls prefer cash.

Time zone

UTC+8 all year round, with no daylight saving time. An 8 pm final in Kuala Lumpur is lunchtime in London.

Language

Malay is the official language, and English is spoken almost everywhere. All signs at the venues are in both.

Weather and health

Hot and humid every day, with short, heavy storms in the late afternoon.

Heat

Drink before you are thirsty, wear a hat and sunscreen at outdoor venues, and plan your sightseeing for the morning. First aid points at every venue have cooling areas.

Storms

Downpours rarely last more than an hour. Carry a compact umbrella or a poncho; outdoor events are paused when there is lightning nearby.

Water and mosquitoes

Drink bottled or filtered water: the venue fountains are filtered. Dengue fever is present in the region, so use insect repellent, especially at dusk.

At the venues

Gates open 90 minutes before the first event, and three hours before the ceremonies. There are security checks at every entrance, and no re-entry.

Allowed

  • One small bag, up to 40 × 30 × 15 cm
  • An empty water bottle
  • Food in a closed container
  • A compact umbrella or a poncho
  • Flags up to 2 × 1 m, without a pole
  • Cameras and phones, for personal use

Not allowed

  • Suitcases and large bags: use the lockers at KL Sentral or Bukit Jalil station
  • Glass, cans and alcohol
  • Smoking and vaping, including outdoors
  • Drones, laser pointers and air horns
  • Selfie sticks and tripods
  • Banners with political or offensive messages

Accessibility

Every venue has wheelchair spaces with a seat for a companion, and companion tickets are free. Book them when you buy your own ticket.

Step-free routes from the nearest station to the gates are signposted in purple. At Bukit Jalil, electric buggies run between the station and the five venues.

Assistance dogs are welcome at all venues, and water bowls are available at the information desks.

Getting around

Leave the car behind: the roads around the venues are closed to traffic on event days.

  • LRT, MRT and Monorail

    Three automated networks serving the Bukit Jalil and city centre venues directly. During the Games, trains run every 3 to 5 minutes until 1 am.

  • KTM Komuter

    Suburban trains to Shah Alam, Subang and Nilai, where free shuttles take over. The pink coaches are reserved for women.

  • Games shuttles

    Free buses for ticket holders from KL Sentral, Semantan, Shah Alam, Subang Jaya, Nilai and Putrajaya Sentral, every 10 minutes from three hours before the first event.

  • Taxis and ride-hailing

    Ride-hailing apps are cheap and widely used. Drop-off points are a 10-minute walk from each venue, as the streets around the gates are closed.

  • On foot

    The heat makes walking tiring. In the centre, a covered, air-conditioned walkway links KLCC to Bukit Bintang in 15 minutes.

  • Paying for transport

    Your event ticket is a free travel pass on the day of your event. On other days, buy a single journey or a stored-value card at any station.

Frequently asked questions

Do children need a ticket?

Children under 2 go in free, on an adult's lap. Older children need their own ticket, at half price up to the age of 16.

What happens if an outdoor event is rained off?

Events continue in light rain and are paused in a thunderstorm. If a session is cancelled and not rescheduled on the same day, your ticket is refunded automatically.

Can I leave a venue and come back?

No, leaving is final. The exception is Putrajaya Lake, where the roadside along the courses is free and open to all.

Is there Wi-Fi at the venues?

Yes, the free “KL2030 Free” network covers every venue, the food villages and the shuttle stations.

I have lost something. Where can I find it?

Go to the venue's information desk on the same day. After 24 hours, items are sent to the central lost property office at KL Sentral, open until 31 August 2030.

Where can I find help during the Games?

Look for the Rimau Crew volunteers in purple shirts, at every venue, station and food village. Between them, they speak more than 40 languages.

How should I dress?

Light clothes are fine at the venues. To visit a mosque or a temple, cover your shoulders and knees and take off your shoes; the National Mosque lends robes at the entrance.
