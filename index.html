A hundred years of Games

Kuala Lumpur hosted the first Games ever held in Asia. Now it welcomes the centenary edition.

  1. 1930

    Hamilton, Canada

    The first British Empire Games: 11 nations, 400 athletes, 6 sports.

  2. 1998

    Kuala Lumpur, Malaysia

    The first Games in Asia. The Bukit Jalil National Stadium opened for the occasion.

  3. 2030

    Kuala Lumpur, Malaysia

    The centenary Games, in the renovated 1998 venues and new locations in the heart of the city.

    Meet Rimau

    Rimau is a Malayan tiger, the country's national animal. It is also an endangered species: fewer than 150 are thought to remain in the wild.

    For every ticket sold, one ringgit goes to a programme protecting the forest where it lives.
